## [1.0.0-beta.1] - 2026-11-14

### Added

Welcome to the first Paintwarden beta, for friends and family! It helps you keep your painting plans and collection in one place.

- Track your paint inventory, wishlist, and substitutes so you know what you already have.
- Organize miniatures with photos, painting stages, recipes, and color schemes.
- Reuse miniature templates and group miniatures into nested Projects, with folders for templates too.
- Add paint catalogs, schemes, and templates through plugins. No paint-brand catalog is bundled.
- Try **Swatch Scan**: tap a miniature photo to find similar paints or schemes. Lighting affects the suggestions, so treat them as a starting point.
- Use the **complementary paint tool** to find a base, complement, highlight, and shadow among paints you own, then save them as a scheme.
- Choose from 13 interface languages.
- Export backups with your photos and restore them when needed. Please keep a backup before trying a new beta.
- Use the desktop app on macOS or Windows, or self-host the browser app with Docker. Desktop apps can connect to their own local vault or an existing server.

### Known limitations

- This is an early preview: expect rough edges and tell us what goes wrong. Keep regular backups outside the app's data folder.
- Some beta downloads may be unsigned. macOS may require **Open Anyway** in Privacy & Security; unsigned Mac builds cannot auto-update. Windows may show a SmartScreen warning. Only open downloads from this project's GitHub Releases page.
- The interface translations have not all had a native-speaker review. Paint names and your own content are not translated.
